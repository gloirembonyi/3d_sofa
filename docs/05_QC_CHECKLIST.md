# Quality-Control Checklist — Caramel Shell 2S

Unit S/N: ________  Date: ________  Inspector: ________

## A. Frame (before upholstery)
- [ ] Timber MC 8–12 % (record: ____ %)
- [ ] Overall 1900 × 900 ± 2 mm; seat-box diagonals equal ± 2 mm
- [ ] All glue lines closed; no squeeze-out left on the spring rails
- [ ] 4 corner blocks + 4 stretcher blocks glued and screwed
- [ ] M8 rail bolts torqued and marked
- [ ] Centre stretcher fitted; the front rail shows no inward bow (straightedge ≤ 1 mm)
- [ ] Frame flat on the table (≤ 1 mm rock)
- [ ] Arm ribs symmetric left/right ± 3 mm (template check)

## B. Suspension
- [ ] 12 springs, arc facing up, centres per the drawing ± 2 mm
- [ ] 24 clips, every clip with a plastic liner, 2 nails each
- [ ] 30 helical ties fitted (3 rows)
- [ ] Crown 22 ± 3 mm at 4 sample springs
- [ ] No spring touches wood under a 100 kg sit load
- [ ] No squeak in the press test (10 presses per bay)
- [ ] Felt insulator + deck pad + decking fitted, no staples through springs
- [ ] Back webbing: 10 vertical + 2 horizontal straps, even tension

## C. Foam and cushions
- [ ] Foam certificates present (density / IFD) for HR40, HR35, HR30
- [ ] Seat inserts 665 × 479 × 139 ± 2 + fibre wrap; crown 14–18 mm
- [ ] Back inserts with lumbar crown; compartment fibre evenly filled
- [ ] Fire-safety labels and conformity per the target market

## D. Upholstery
- [ ] Pile direction consistent on all panels
- [ ] No pleats or puckers on the arm fronts and rims
- [ ] Seams straight, hidden in the pile; no loose threads
- [ ] Base band line straight ± 2 mm; ends tucked into the arm noses
- [ ] Zips run smoothly; zip pulls hidden
- [ ] Back cushions retained by hook-and-loop

## E. Legs and underside
- [ ] 4 oak legs tight, no wobble, finish undamaged
- [ ] Felt glides fitted
- [ ] Centre foot set 1 mm clear of a flat floor
- [ ] Dust cover stapled at 30 mm pitch, taut, no tears

## F. Function and appearance
- [ ] Sofa level on a flat floor, no rocking
- [ ] Sit test 2 persons, 5 min: no noise, no bottoming out, even support
- [ ] Rise test: seat front edge firm enough to stand up easily
- [ ] Seat cushions level ± 3 mm, centre gap even
- [ ] Colour match between panels under daylight (D65)
- [ ] Final photographs taken (front, 3/4, side, rear)

## G. Periodic / type-test items (not per unit)
- [ ] EN 12520 / EN 1728 strength and fatigue (lab report no. ______)
- [ ] EN 1022 stability (lab report no. ______)
- [ ] Spring load–deflection curve from the supplier
- [ ] Fabric: Martindale, pilling, seam slippage, fire
