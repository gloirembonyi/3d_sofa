What's under the bouclé

Made the way a good upholstery workshop works: layer by layer, from a hardwood frame.

  • 01
    Kiln-dried beech and birch-ply frameDowelled, glued and bolted rails, with corner blocks at every seat joint. CNC-cut ribs give the shell its shape.
  • 02
    12 sinuous steel springs8-gauge springs on plastic-lined clips, tied together. A centre brace keeps the frame square.
  • 03
    Quiet, felt-lined deckFelt and a foam pad sit between the springs and the cushions, so the seat stays quiet.
  • 04
    Layered foamAn HR40 seat core with an HR35 comfort top. Soft HR30 back cushions with lumbar shaping.

    Fibre wrap and cotton tickingEvery cushion insert has its own inner cover, so the bouclé covers zip off.

Quick room check

Allow about 2.3 m of wall, plus 45 cm in front for a coffee table. The sofa is 900 mm deep, so measure doorways and stair turns before delivery.

Living with it

Easy to keep looking new

Zip-off cushion covers

Seat and back covers unzip for cleaning. Flip and plump the cushions every week or two to keep them even.

Bouclé care

Vacuum gently with a brush head. Blot spills straight away and don't rub. A fabric shaver lifts any pilling.

Floor-friendly

Felt glides protect wooden floors. The legs unscrew, which makes moving and delivery easier.
